CCTV Playback Workspace

A sanitized overview of a private operator workspace for reviewing multi-camera footage, filtering by time, previewing clips, and exporting evidence-ready video segments.

What needed to improve

Problem

Operators needed a faster way to locate footage across several cameras without manually hunting through folders, guessing time windows, or repeating export steps.

Build

The workspace focuses on day/time filtering, camera selection, preview playback, merged clip export, and a local-first workflow that can run on the operator machine.

Result

The case study demonstrates operational automation experience without exposing camera locations, internal paths, recordings, or private repository details.
